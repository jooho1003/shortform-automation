# 시놉시스

작은 부엉이가 새벽 숲에서 눈을 뜨고, 이슬 맺힌 잎을 건드려 물방울을 마시고, 첫 햇살을 향해 날아오른다.
대사 없음. 환경음과 효과음만으로 진행. 마지막 0.5초 무음 뒤 로고.

## 락 블록

### CHARACTER
Small round owl, cream chest feathers, dark brown wings, oversized amber eyes, tiny curved beak, two feather tufts. Consistent proportions: head 40% of body height.

### COSTUME
No clothing. A single green leaf tucked behind the left ear tuft — must appear in every shot.

### GLOBAL STYLE
Soft 3D animated look, pastel dawn palette (peach, mint, lavender), volumetric morning light, shallow depth of field, 9:16 vertical.

### NEGATIVE
No text, no watermark, no humans, no extra animals, no photorealism, no harsh shadows.
